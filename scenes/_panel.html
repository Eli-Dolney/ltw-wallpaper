Changes apply live and are saved per scene. Edit the scene file for anything not listed here.
Ltw Wallpaper
The menu bar tint works by setting the system wallpaper to a snapshot of the scene or a color; macOS tints the menu bar from it. "Restore Original Wallpaper" is under Advanced in the menu.
macOS Dock
These are the Dock's own preferences; the Dock restarts about a second after a change (it blinks once). Spacers are removed by dragging them out of the Dock. macOS does not offer a way to hide the Dock's background tray.
Volume mixer
Apps appear while they play audio and stay listed while adjusted. An app is only touched once its slider leaves 100% (double-click a slider to return to 100%). Above 100% is a boost. The first adjustment asks for the “System Audio Recording” permission; nothing is recorded.
